## How tools get listed

- **Live product only.** No waitlists, no "coming soon."
- **Public pricing or a real free tier.** If a vendor is enterprise-sales-only, we still list it, tagged `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`, so you know what you're getting into before you book a demo.
- **Actually used for marketing**, not a general-purpose tool with one relevant use case bolted on.
- **Reviewed quarterly.** Dead links and abandoned products are removed — see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **No pay-to-list.** Sponsored placements are labeled `Sponsored` and never displace a better-fit free entry — see [Sponsors](#sponsors).

*Last full review: August 2026.*

## AI Tools for SEO Content Briefs & Optimization

*AI tools for SEO content briefs turn a target keyword into a SERP-backed outline and score drafts against what's already ranking.*

- **[Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io)** — Grades content against highest-ranking pages and recommends terms and topics to add. `Paid`
- **[Frase](https://www.frase.io)** — Builds SEO content briefs and outlines from live SERP data, then scores drafts against them. `Paid`
- **[MarketMuse](https://www.marketmuse.com)** — AI content planning platform that scores drafts and flags topic-coverage gaps versus competitors. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[NeuronWriter](https://neuronwriter.com)** — Analyzes highest-ranking pages for NLP-derived terms and gives writers a content score to target. `Paid`
- **[Outranking](https://www.outranking.io)** — Generates SERP-based outlines and AI drafts with checklists for on-page SEO. `Paid`
- **[Scalenut](https://www.scalenut.com)** — Combines keyword clustering, SEO briefs, and AI drafting for content teams. `Paid`
- **[Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com)** — Content Editor scores drafts against live SERP data with on-page optimization guidelines. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Social Media Scheduling

*AI social schedulers draft captions, suggest hashtags, and time cross-platform posts automatically.*

- **[Buffer](https://buffer.com)** — Schedules and publishes posts across social channels with a built-in AI caption assistant. `Freemium`
- **[FeedHive](https://feedhive.com)** — AI scheduler with post recycling, hashtag generation, and predictive optimal-send-time suggestions. `Paid`
- **[Later](https://later.com)** — Visual content calendar built around Instagram-first planning with AI caption generation. `Freemium`
- **[Publer](https://publer.com)** — Schedules and auto-publishes across networks with an AI assistant for captions and hashtags. `Freemium`
- **[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com)** — Category-based post scheduling with AI caption generation and content recycling. `Paid`
- **[Vista Social](https://vistasocial.com)** — AI-powered scheduling, listening, and engagement command center for social teams. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Ad Copywriting & Creative Generation

*These platforms generate and test ad copy, banners, and creative variants for paid social and search campaigns.*

- **[Ad Multiplier](https://admultipliers.com/)** — Makes visual variations of existing 4–30 second video ads for marketers, with optional original audio and MP4 downloads. `Paid`
- **[AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai)** — Generates ad creative and copy variants for paid social and search campaigns from brand assets. `Paid`
- **[Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai)** — AI copywriting workflows for ad copy, social captions, and marketing content generation. `Freemium`
- **[Jacquard](https://www.jacquard.com)** — Enterprise AI platform (formerly Phrasee) that generates and optimizes on-brand marketing language across channels. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Omneky](https://www.omneky.com)** — Generates and tests ad creative variants across Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn from brand assets. `Paid`
- **[Pencil](https://trypencil.com)** — Generates static and video ad creative from brand assets and connects directly to major ad platforms. `Paid`
- **[Persado](https://www.persado.com)** — Enterprise AI platform that generates and optimizes motivation-based marketing copy for regulated brands. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[Smartly.io](https://www.smartly.io)** — AI-powered creative automation and campaign management for social and programmatic advertising. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`
- **[The Brief](https://www.thebrief.ai)** — AI ad creation platform (formerly Creatopy) for designing, resizing, and publishing multi-format ad sets. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

- **[Mailmodo](https://www.mailmodo.com)** — Generates AI email campaign strategies, templates, and interactive AMP emails. `Freemium`
- **[Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com)** — Ecommerce email and SMS automation with an AI assistant for drafting full email campaigns. `Freemium`

- **[Upfluence](https://www.upfluence.com)** — Influencer search, outreach, and payments platform with modular, custom-quoted pricing. `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`

## AI Tools for Short-Form Marketing Video

*These tools turn scripts, footage, or long-form video into short, captioned clips for social distribution.*

- **[HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com)** — Generates AI avatar videos with voice translation and lip-sync for marketing and social content. `Freemium`
- **[InVideo](https://invideo.io)** — AI video generator that produces marketing videos from prompts or scripts with stock media. `Freemium`
- **[Munch](https://www.getmunch.com)** — Repurposes long-form video into short, captioned clips optimized for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. `Paid`
- **[Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro)** — Turns long-form video into short clips with AI-selected highlights, captions, and reframing. `Freemium`
- **[Pictory](https://pictory.ai)** — Turns scripts or long-form video into short branded video content with AI voiceovers and captions. `Freemium`
- **[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io)** — Generates AI avatar presenter videos from text scripts for marketing and training content. `Freemium`

- **[Landingi](https://landingi.com)** — Landing page builder with AI content suggestions, A/B testing, and smart sections. `Paid`

- **[Unbounce](https://unbounce.com)** — Landing page builder with AI copywriting and Smart Traffic that routes visitors to highest-converting variants. `Paid`
- **[VWO](https://vwo.com)** — A/B testing and website personalization platform for conversion rate optimization. `Paid`
